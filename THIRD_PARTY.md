## Images

- **Character portraits** (`assets/portraits/char_*.png`, embedded in the DLL): in-game screenshots of the 21 ACP characters
  in *Assassin's Creed Origins*, taken by the project author and cut out from the background. Assassin's Creed and its
  characters are © Ubisoft Entertainment; the images are used only to show the choice in this free, non-commercial fan mod.
- **Portrait backdrop** (`assets/portraits/backdrop_origins.png`): generated by the project's branding tools (not
  included); the hieroglyphs are drawn with Noto Sans Egyptian Hieroglyphs (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
